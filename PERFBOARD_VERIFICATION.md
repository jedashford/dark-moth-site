# R6 carrier instruction verification

The current build is the **ESP32-C3 SuperMini R6 carrier**: an 18-column × 24-row isolated-pad board, 33 component assemblies, four raised purchased modules and three detachable external plugs. [CARRIER_GUIDE.md](CARRIER_GUIDE.md) gives the assembly sequence; [PRODUCT_ELECTRICAL_PLAN.md](PRODUCT_ELECTRICAL_PLAN.md) records circuit decisions and outstanding physical qualification.

The earlier latch, driver and combined-board views remain historical circuit references. They do not define the R6 build. In particular, R5 battery-negative routing, TP4056/MT3608 modules, BS170 drivers and direct battery-level button sensing must not be mixed with R6.

## One source for every hole

[electronics/perfboard-layout.json](electronics/perfboard-layout.json) records component pins, nets, modules, adapter footprints and external contacts. The interactive carrier, top/bottom worksheets and CSV exports derive from it. Hole names stay fixed when the solder-side view is mirrored.

The five MOSFET driver groups form repeated rows. Each has its own 100 Ω gate resistor and 10 kΩ pulldown. Their source legs share ground; the five drain returns remain separate. The regulated 5 V and 12 V positives remain separate. The six-pin lights plug carries +12 V, G, R, B, WW and CW. LED return contacts are switched outputs, not ground contacts.

The SOT23 devices use seven Adafruit1230 adapter boards. The power adapter holds two MOSFETs; each of the five LED adapters holds one. A separate adapter carries the TLV803EA30DBZR voltage supervisor, whose GND/RESET/VDD pins differ from MOSFET G/S/D pins. The published adapter pad map determines lead placement. Two retail packs supply the seven boards needed for one device. The remaining through-hole transistors retain explicit role labels; check the purchased manufacturer's pin order before bending their legs.

The firmware uses HOLD GPIO3, isolated active-LOW BUTTON GPIO4, and G/R/B/WW/CW GPIO5/6/7/10/20. The first power-on press is consumed. A later two-second hold arms shutdown, which occurs on release. [FIRMWARE_GUIDE.md](FIRMWARE_GUIDE.md) covers upload isolation and operation.

## Independent checks

[The electrical report](electronics/verification.json) is generated by `tools/verify_r6_electronics.py`. It checks exact component values and roles, occupied-hole ownership, net continuity, independent supply and signal nets, C3 GPIO assignments, service-shunt isolation, adapter pad mapping, nominal body clearances, module height, voltage-supervisor routing and external connector connections. Deliberate-fault tests prove that swapped adapter pins, missing returns, bridged supplies, bypassed service isolation and missing or miswired voltage supervision fail verification.

Python worksheet tests compare every leg, solder junction and terminal with canonical data. Node tests use the vendored Three.js geometry to check lead endpoints, adapter contacts, carrier state, module placement and viewer controls. They do not establish real solder clearance or supplier dimensions.

The browser test starts its own temporary HTTP server and exercises the actual GLB, repeated vertical rotation, drag release, fixed top/bottom views, component selection, cumulative assembly steps, module choices, load-error recovery, desktop/mobile layout and downloads. [The browser record](preview/site/perfboard/browser-checks.json) includes source hashes and the individual results. [R6 product evidence](preview/site/r6/browser-checks.json) covers the complete case and print guide.

## Physical checks still required

The reports explicitly record **physical validation false**. The nominal blank is 47.78 × 63.02 mm with 2.54 mm hole pitch; an 18 × 24 hole count alone does not establish outside dimensions or edge margins. Measure the actual board before printing its fitted holder.

The C3 clone, Pololu modules, connector kit, formed leads and solder envelopes remain unmeasured. Source-derived adapter pad coordinates are distinguished from illustrative purchased-part geometry. The generic charger’s actual pad assignments and dimensions need confirmation on the purchased board. Unverified physical module pads are not assigned guessed signal names. Follow the module silkscreen and published pinout, then verify continuity before power.

The battery keeps its built-in protection. Its factory 1.25 mm plug may be replaced with the available two-pin XH-style plug one insulated conductor at a time. Project contact numbers and measured polarity define the connection; wire colors and similar-looking housings do not prove polarity or compatibility. No rating is inferred for the generic kit.

Charging uses complete manual shutdown before connecting USB; there is no automatic USB lockout or simultaneous operation. B− remains separate from OUT− system ground.

The first build requires an unpowered continuity check, current-limited rail bring-up, latch/button tests, full-load voltage and temperature measurements, standby-current measurement and an actual case fit trial. Charging is limited to the supervised open-case test conditions in the electrical guide until the pack's limits, charge current and thermal behavior are qualified. No batch-production, unattended-charge or battery-runtime claim is made by the digital checks.

## Reproduce

From the enclosure directory, using the dependencies in [README.md](README.md#reproduce):

```sh
python tools/build_perfboard_layout.py
python tools/build_perfboard_worksheets.py
python tools/verify_r6_electronics.py
python tools/build_guide.py
python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_perfboard_*.py' -v
python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_r6_electronics.py' -v
node --test tests/test_perfboard_carrier.mjs tests/test_perfboard_adapters.mjs tests/test_perfboard_state.mjs tests/test_perfboard_geometry.mjs tests/test_perfboard_connections.mjs tests/test_perfboard_controls.mjs tests/test_electronics_overview.mjs
node tests/test_perfboard_browser.mjs
```

The browser test requires Playwright and Chromium; `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` may identify an existing installation. It does not use the development or production server and does not contact hardware.
